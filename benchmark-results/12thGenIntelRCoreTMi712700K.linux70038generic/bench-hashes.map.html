How fast BLAKE3 servil runs, beside other hashes

Each small chart is one measurement: its row is what a program hashes, its column how it calls. Point at a row's or a column's name to see what it means. Open a chart to read its values under the pointer or where you tap; a hash's name below a title hides or shows its lines, and the switch at the top shows each hash as a rate (bytes per second; knob up, higher is better) or a cost (nanoseconds per byte; knob down, lower is better), per second the program waits (wall-clock), per second of processor time over all its threads (CPU, dotted), or per joule the CPU package used (energy, dashed).
