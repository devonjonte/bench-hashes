About the hashes compared

BLAKE3 official is the blake3 crate, the reference implementation; BLAKE3 servil is this fork of it, with the same digests. SHA-256 is the fastest implementation measured on each machine (the sha2 crate, ring, or the operating system's). sha1sum and md5sum, drawn in grey, compute SHA-1 and MD5: both are broken as cryptographic hashes (collisions are cheap to make), and they appear here only because many people still run them as checksums; a comparison with them is a comparison with the tool in hand, not with a safe alternative.

Each bar is one run's mean, by the same rule as the map (clocks::summary), on a machine whose load reading is on its map; no verdict is drawn across runs. From johnservil/bench-hashes, measuring Devon Jonte's line of BLAKE3 servil; the cells shown are FROZEN.md's "The overview".
