## Interpretation fixed before collection

Compare old/new and each side's repeat for every point/state/share in every
cohort. Busy/unobserved output remains descriptive; abstention stays in the
record. Target gains are supported where both corresponding pair fast ratios
are below0.90 and repeat variation is smaller than the gain. Other states and
shares stay visible; no lone pooled median can certify the entire call.

A queue cost recurring on the new side but absent from same-code controls
remains a candidate cost. If a declared affinity makes corresponding states
and repeats stable, report that exact control and its magnitude as user
placement guidance, while keeping default-placement costs visible. If neither
happens, keep the problem open; no general no-regression claim or promotion.
Frequency/counter data describe calling threads, not unobserved worker cores
or whole-process energy. No sole physical cause inferred from these controls.
